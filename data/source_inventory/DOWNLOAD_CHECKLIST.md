## Important source issues

- Distinct URLs may contain duplicate documents. Several project lists appear in different upload folders or versions. Compare contents before merging or counting records.
- The publications page labels an audit opinion as 2024, but its filename says 2025. Confirm the reporting year inside the document.
- Some 2022–2024 project labels contain a separate hyperlink pointing to the 2025 combined list. The checklist preserves actual URLs and flags the older proposed lists by filename.
- Upload folder dates are not necessarily the reporting year. Several titles are incomplete or inconsistent; labels below come from the council website.
- Dedicated ward development committee rosters and a standalone LGEF utilisation report were not identified in the inspected pages. Check the IDP, financial statements and minutes for this information; record any remaining gaps.
